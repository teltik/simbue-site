Why real cellular beats a virtual number

VoIP APIs rent you a number that lives in the cloud. Simbue gives you a line on a real mobile network, so it works like the phone in your pocket because it is one.

Every call passes through Simbue, which decides where it rings, keeps the call log and protects it. Pick a ring order per number; switch any time.

Optional: Guardian AI

Add AI when you want it. Ours or your own.

Simbue works fully without AI. When you want it, turn on Guardian, our AI that hears a scam and ends the call while it is happening, or connect your own AI platform through SIP, webhooks and the API.

  • Off by default: no AI unless you switch it on, per number
  • Bring your own AI: send calls to your AI voice agent over SIP and texts to your server
  • Guardian rules in plain English for calls, texts, or both
  • Start in Log only to see what it would catch, then switch to Enforce
  • Hold suspicious texts and text an alert to family or a caretaker
  • Works on PBX calls too: listening starts when the cell answers

API reference

API keys

Create keys per environment or per team member in API & webhooks. Send the key as a Bearer token or as X-API-Key. Rotating gives a new key and stops the old one immediately.

Event webhook

Set one endpoint URL and choose the events you want, or leave all selected. Each delivery is signed with your secret so you can verify it came from Simbue. Send a test event, see recent deliveries, pause without losing settings.

# Quick start curl https://streaming.kosherphone.com/v1/lines \ -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_…"

API reference

The Guardian API lets any channel you run, voice or text, use Simbue's detection and action engine. Open a session when a call or thread starts, stream in what each side says, and Simbue judges it against the line's active rules and tells you what to do.

Webhook events

SIP trunk for your PBX

Simbue recognises your PBX by its IP address, so the trunk needs no username, password or registration. Up to 5 PBX addresses per account. Only your PBX can use your numbers, and only with your own Simbue numbers as caller ID.

Works with FreePBX, 3CX, FusionPBX, Asterisk, Yeastar and any PBX that supports SIP trunks.

A call your PBX sends back to the cell rings only the cell, shows the original caller with carrier caller verification, and is billed once. An X-Simbue-Call header lets your PBX match calls exactly.
